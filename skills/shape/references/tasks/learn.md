# Reader task — `learn`

The reader does not yet have the model. Everything is new, so everything costs.

**Target shape** — worked example first, then the generalisation. A figure and
its label are contiguous.

**Signature rule** — ⛔ never state the same content in prose *and* diagram. The
redundancy that helps a `locate` reader costs a `learn` reader working memory.

## Allowed transforms

- Move a worked example ahead of the abstract statement it illustrates.
- Bring a figure and its caption/label adjacent; split a figure that carries two
  ideas.
- Delete prose that restates an adjacent diagram — keep the diagram, or keep the
  prose, and say in the report which was kept.
- Introduce a term at first use rather than three sections later.

## Forbidden transforms

- ⛔ Removing an example to save space. Examples are the payload.
- ⛔ Front-loading definitions before the reader has a reason to want them.
- ⛔ Adding a summary that duplicates the worked example.
- ⛔ Splitting a figure from its explanation across a page or section boundary.

## Acceptance questions

1. Does the first concrete example appear before the first abstraction?
2. Is every figure adjacent to its label and explained exactly once?
3. Is every term used after it is introduced, not before?
4. Did any example disappear?
